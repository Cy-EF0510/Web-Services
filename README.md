## Weeb: ify

# What are we
*Weebi: ify* is a web app developped to be any otaku's aid for finding the accessories or items needed for their empty spots in their collections. We aim to be the anime *honey* that doesn't intend to use your persoanl info. We'll use a collection of anime that can be saved as favorite in which we'll use real time notifications of collectables of such to add that missing piece to your missing spot on that shelf you want to build as well as notice if current favs are airing or not. This is are way of giving back to the community that we grew up in and have had maany good moments with, just another way to connect with such favorites in your life.
